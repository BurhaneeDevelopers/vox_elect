# VoxElect Response Templates

## How to Use

These templates guide Elara's response structure for common query types. Templates are suggestions — Elara adapts language naturally while maintaining structure.

---

## Template 1: Process Explanation (e.g., "How does voting work?")

```markdown
## [Process Name]

[1-2 sentence intro that contextualizes why this process matters]

**Here's how it works, step by step:**

1. **[Step Name]** — [Brief description, 1-2 sentences]
2. **[Step Name]** — [Brief description]
3. **[Step Name]** — [Brief description]
...

> 📍 **Good to know:** [Any important caveat or state-specific note]

*Source: [Official source name]*

---
💡 *Want to know more? You might ask: "[Follow-up question]"*
```

## Template 2: Voter Registration Info

```markdown
## Voter Registration in [State]

To vote in [State], you'll need to register by **[deadline]**.

**Registration options:**
1. **Online** — [state registration URL] (fastest option)
2. **By mail** — Download the [National Voter Registration Form](https://www.vote.gov)
3. **In person** — At your local [DMV / election office / library]

> ⚠️ **Deadline:** [X days away / specific date]

**You'll need:**
- [Requirement 1]
- [Requirement 2]

*Source: [State] Secretary of State via Vote.gov*

---
💡 *Want to know more? You might ask: "What ID do I need to vote in [State]?"*
```

## Template 4: Ballot Measure Explainer

```markdown
## [Measure Name/Number]

**What it is:** [Type — Constitutional Amendment / Statute / Bond Measure]

**What it would do:**
[2-3 sentence neutral description of what the measure would change or enact]

**Official arguments filed:**
- **In favor:** [Summary of official pro argument, if available]
- **In opposition:** [Summary of official con argument, if available]

> 📍 **Important:** These are official arguments filed by campaigns — not my recommendations. How you vote is entirely your choice.

*Source: [State] Official Voter Guide / Ballotpedia*

---
💡 *Want to know more? You might ask: "Who sponsored this measure?"*
```
